# Survey 2 — DEEP ("How You Really Travel" · ~8 min · for close friends)

**Form title:** How You Really Travel — the longer version

**Description:**
You're getting the long one because I trust your take. ~8 min on how you actually experience
trips — the messy real stuff, not the highlight reel. Anonymous unless you add your name at the
end. Skip anything that doesn't apply. Thank you!

**Tip (for building in Google Forms):** use Section breaks between each block and turn on
"go to section based on answer" for Q2 (kids/family) so solo travelers skip the family block.
Keeps it feeling short.
